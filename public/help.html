Help

Quick answers about your score, your report and your emails. Anything else, email hello@digitalwellbeingscore.app.

I paid, but I can't find my report

After payment you are returned to a page with a download link. We also email a confirmation with the same download button to the address you paid with. Check your spam or promotions folder, and search for “Digital Wellbeing Score”. The link in that email keeps working, so you can come back to it later.

Still stuck? Email us from the address you paid with and we'll resend it.

The download link says something went wrong

Download links are generated fresh each time you click and expire after a few minutes, so just click the button again. If it keeps failing, email us and we'll sort it out.

I didn't get my results email

Your results email is sent when you first sign up with your email address. Check spam or promotions, and add hello@digitalwellbeingscore.app to your contacts. If you have signed up before with the same address, it will not be sent a second time.

How do I retake the quiz?

Go to digitalwellbeingscore.app and take the quiz again. If you sign up with the same email address, we'll show how your score has changed since last time.

How do I stop the emails?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any of our emails. It takes effect straight away. Purchase confirmations are not marketing emails and are still sent after a purchase.

Can I get a refund?

If your report didn't arrive or won't open, email us from the address you paid with and we'll send it to you directly. If you're not satisfied with it, email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full. See our terms for details.

What happens to my data?

See our privacy policy. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing us.

Contact

Email hello@digitalwellbeingscore.app. We aim to reply within two working days.
