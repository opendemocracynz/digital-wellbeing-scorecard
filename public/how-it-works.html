How the Digital Wellbeing Score works

The scorecard is built around a simple idea: lasting change starts with seeing your habits clearly, and grows when you have the reasons, the know-how and the support to act on them.

We looked at the research on how technology use relates to sleep, attention, mood and relationships, and turned the themes that kept coming up into 15 everyday questions. Whether you've never given your screen habits a second thought, or you're already looking for practical ways to improve, the scorecard is designed to meet you where you are.

  1. Step 1

    Notice

    Each question describes a real, ordinary habit: where your phone sleeps, what you do with five spare minutes, how you eat your meals. You aren't being tested. Reading the options is often the first time anyone notices a pattern they'd stopped seeing, and for some people that alone is enough to start shifting things.

  2. Step 2

    Care enough to change

    Your score and archetype put a name and a number to what you noticed. The point isn't to judge you. It's to make the question “do I want this to be different?” a real one, and to give you something to measure against. Wanting it is what everything else rests on.

  3. Step 3

    Know what to try

    Your four-pillar breakdown shows where small changes are likely to pay off most: your body (Physiological), your mind (Psychological), your relationships (Social) and your thinking (Cognitive). Many of the best first moves are things we already know we should do. What's often missing is a clear picture of which ones matter for you, and a nudge. Our emails share quick wins you can try straight away.

  4. Step 4

    Put it into practice

    Knowing isn't the same as doing. The full report goes deeper than the quiz: it looks closely at your archetype and what the research shows, and gives you a 7-day challenge, with concrete steps to take one day at a time. It's a small one-time purchase. We think putting a little value on change helps people take it seriously, and the report is there to make following through easier.

  5. Step 5

    Make it stick

    Habits slip back when nobody is looking. That's why we invite you to retake the scorecard after a month and again after three. Seeing your score move, or not, shows you what's working, and a small win is a good reason to keep going. Retaking is free, and the printable poster we send at three months is our thank-you.
