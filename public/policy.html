Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

The Digital Wellbeing Score (digitalwellbeingscore.app) is run by Legitimise This! (formerly Open Democracy NZ) (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • Quiz answers and scores. When you finish the quiz we save your answers, total score, archetype and pillar scores for research and to show your result.
  • Email address and first name (optional). If you sign up, we save your email so we can send your results and follow-up emails. Your quiz result is then linked to that email address, including any later retakes.
  • Purchase details. If you buy a report, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe gives us your email, the report you bought and confirmation that payment succeeded. Stripe also collects billing details such as your name and address in order to calculate tax.
  • Site usage. We use Google Analytics to see how people use the site (pages viewed, quiz progress, general location and device type). It uses cookies or similar identifiers.

How we use it

  • To show your result and email you your results.
  • To deliver reports you buy and send you a purchase confirmation.
  • To send follow-up emails: check-ins over roughly three months suggesting you retake the quiz and see your progress. Every one has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand digital wellbeing patterns and improve the quiz. Research findings are published only as grouped statistics, never in a way that identifies you.
  • To meet our tax and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted services to run the site. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Stripe – payments and tax calculation
  • Supabase – database and file storage
  • Netlify – website hosting
  • MailerSend – sending email
  • Google Analytics – site usage statistics

These providers may store or process information outside New Zealand, including in the United States and India. We choose providers with appropriate security practices, but their handling of your information is also subject to their own policies and the laws where they operate.

How long we keep it

We keep quiz and subscriber information until you ask us to delete it, or until it is no longer needed for the purposes above. Payment and tax records are kept for as long as New Zealand law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email.
  • Ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected.
  • Ask us to delete your information. We will do so unless we must keep something by law.

Email hello@digitalwellbeingscore.app to do any of this. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Security

Data is stored with access controls and sent over encrypted connections. No online service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the site.
