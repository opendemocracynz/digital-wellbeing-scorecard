# Digital Wellbeing Scorecard Marketing Plan

## Goal

Launch the Digital Wellbeing Scorecard by 2026-08-18 with a low-cost, high-learning campaign that drives email signups and a small number of paid report purchases without burning through Netlify credits.

## Constraints

- Netlify budget is tight, so keep deployment volume low and avoid unnecessary rebuilds.
- The launch window is short, so prioritise a simple paid channel with clear tracking.
- The offer should stay focused on the quiz, the results email, and the paid report as a follow-up offer.

## Recommended launch channel

### Priority: Meta ads (Facebook + Instagram)

Meta is the best fit for this launch because:
- the quiz is visual, emotionally resonant, and easy to explain in a short ad,
- the audience is broad enough to test quickly, and
- the creative can be reused across Facebook and Instagram without much extra work.

### Secondary option: Google Search

Use Google Search only if you already have a small amount of keyword data or a local audience willing to test. It is usually better as a second wave once the landing page and message are validated.

## Campaign concept

### Core message

"Find out how technology is shaping your attention, mood, and routines — and get a practical next step."

### Offer structure

1. Free quiz
2. Free results email with a short interpretation
3. Optional paid full report for users who want deeper guidance

## Initial campaign brief

### Audience

- Adults aged 22–45
- People interested in productivity, mindfulness, digital habits, parenting, work-life balance, and wellbeing
- Broad interest targeting, not narrow mental-health targeting

### Creative angle

Use a simple, non-judgmental message:
- "Is your phone running your life?"
- "Take the 2-minute scan and see your digital wellbeing score"

### Suggested ad variants

- Variant A: Problem-led
  - Hook: "Is your phone stealing your attention?"
  - CTA: "Take the scorecard"
- Variant B: Benefit-led
  - Hook: "Understand your digital habits in 2 minutes"
  - CTA: "Start the quiz"
- Variant C: Social proof-style
  - Hook: "Most people don’t realise how often their attention is being pulled away"
  - CTA: "Find out your score"

## Landing page setup

Use the existing quiz page as the primary destination.

Recommended tracking parameters:
- utm_source=meta
- utm_medium=paid_social
- utm_campaign=attention-reset
- utm_content=carousel-1

These should be appended to the landing URL so GA4 and the quiz analytics can attribute signups correctly.

## Budget and timing

### Suggested spend plan

- Start with a modest test budget:
  - $10–$20/day for 5–7 days
- Keep the campaign narrow enough to learn quickly without overcommitting

### Recommended schedule

- August 2–8: prepare creative, tracking links, and landing-page QA
- August 9–15: launch the first test campaign
- August 16–18: review conversion quality, pause underperformers, and refine
- August 18: use the best-performing creative and message as the launch version

## Conversion goals

Track three signals:
1. Quiz starts
2. Email signups
3. Paid report clicks or purchases

The key success metric for the first wave is not revenue; it is signups with usable email addresses and a clear signal that the message resonates.

## Messaging guardrails

- Keep the language practical and calm
- Avoid medical claims or over-promising transformation
- Make it clear that the quiz is a simple assessment, not a diagnosis
- Use the same message across ad, landing page, and follow-up email to avoid confusion

## Follow-up plan

After the first wave:
- review email signup rate,
- compare which creative drove the best completion rate,
- keep one clear winner for the next phase,
- then expand to a second creative or a small Search campaign if the first test proves useful.

## Recommended next actions

1. Create one short video or static image set for Meta.
2. Add a simple UTM-tagged landing URL for the paid campaign.
3. Test one primary message for 5–7 days.
4. Review signup and completion data before increasing spend.
